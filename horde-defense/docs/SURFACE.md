## Direction contract

THESIS: An inhabited checkpoint under pressure. The battlefield owns the screen; the line visibly connects every breach to the one stationary survivor.

OWN-WORLD: Worn olive asphalt, dusk teal steel, parchment text and amber warning paint. Barlow Condensed display lettering, compact legible body text. Squared utility controls, original painted survivor/enemy/fortification sprites, subdued ground material and regional panoramas. Code-native overlays preserve tactical clarity.

STORY: Choose an infected country, prepare one survivor, defend, retain earnings, clear the country and open its neighbors. Show live actions and why they happened.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large Lastlight title with checkpoint scene on the menu; world map and selected-country dossier on campaign; eleven-lane arena fills the combat center below health/ammo, with tactical controls and short NPC log beside it. Primary deployment action stays next to readiness.

FORM: Field outpost / service-road defense, candidate 5 in the grounded direction set (survival journal, civil-defense poster, depot inventory, evacuation radio, field outpost, geographic atlas, equipment manual).

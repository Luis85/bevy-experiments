## Player guidance and commercial decisions

The existing Activities journey continues to connect housing, supplies, transport and contribution with the actual ownership and economy rules. This pass addresses a later decision: whether a company route is worth attempting. Fleet's “Before dispatch” explanation shows the first departure, requested load, visible queue or stock, full-load receipt and the payroll time that receipt could cover. For warehouse sales it compares the buyer's payment with a named visible retail seller and exposes a merchandise shortfall before payroll or travel. Missing information remains unknown. [Planning API and tests](../crates/terra-sandbox/src/transport/company/planning.rs) and the [company guide](COMPANIES.md) define these meanings.

The preview is read-only. It reserves neither goods nor public money, and payroll coverage is not a predicted journey time or profit. The native review showed three requested passengers and a 12.64-credit full-load receipt, qualified by “only after funded delivery.” The player can connect the proposed work to the persisted Finances report instead of treating a fare as earnings. This helps sustained decisions, but does not establish campaign-length content, human onboarding success or retention.

The quantity review uncovered a concrete usability gap: the old native cycle skipped three, so the compact career could not be configured at its full existing capacity. The passenger cycle now includes three and 30, with freight choices retained. A native integration test creates and assigns the three-passenger service through the same UI actions and authority commands. The candidate rendered journey also reached that choice. See [Fleet](../crates/terra-demo/src/fleet.rs) and [guidance](GUIDANCE.md).

### Finite one-hour passenger career

The new [career scenario](../crates/terra-sandbox/tests/company_career.rs) starts with the ordinary 250.00-credit owner balance, uses a rented compact, registers a company, hires a driver and funds work through ordinary commands. It does not use the starter vehicle or add further owner funding. Existing roads, prices, queues and public money are retained.

The final frozen-source run completed **65 deliveries over 3,600 service seconds**. Finishing the final load and physically returning the rental brought total elapsed time to **3,649.5 seconds**. Receipts were **821.60 credits**. Known cash costs were registration 20.00, recruitment 10.00, payroll 70.71, leases 23.40, fuel net of refund 2.38 and return damage 0.63: **127.12 credits total**. Combined owner and company cash therefore increased by **694.48 credits**.

Six exact JSON resumes at ten-minute intervals and a regional accounting roundtrip check continuity. The scenario conserves its money, escrow and passenger population. Five explicit ten-second wait-and-resume interventions were required; this is managed service, not unattended autonomy. Ecology time is zero, the owner's living and management time is unpriced, and public funding is finite. The persisted company ledger's result after its recorded owner costs is 699.29 credits; the external cash reconciliation additionally includes 4.81 credits of pre-assignment lease, net fuel and return damage. The distinction is disclosed rather than inventing historical ledger entries. Fresh evidence: [novice career](verification/v0.13-final/novice-career-report.json). The final frozen-source run reproduced these figures exactly.

The two warehouse careers remain essential counterexamples. Contributed goods leave a **−84.69-credit** result after known owner-paid costs; buying stock at retail leaves **−161.49 credits**, with acquisition labor and other unrecorded inputs still unpriced. Their prices and accounting treatment are unchanged. One viable finite passenger strategy does not make every route profitable or establish an indefinitely replenished economy. Fresh evidence: [contributed career](verification/v0.13-final/career-report.json) and [purchased career](verification/v0.13-final/purchased-career-report.json).

## Walking, vehicles and physical recovery

The prior native review retained a 261.8 m walking route because a parked pickup blocked the direct corridor. The new planner considers deterministic two-corner bypasses around stopped bodies, preferring the shorter valid side. A proposal must improve the old route, keep the complete bypass within 160 m, fit the existing waypoint limit and pass source, facility, structure and body checks. The shared 16,384-query budget remains; unsupported or exhausted proposals retain the previous route proposal. Actual movement still passes through the ordinary resolver. Planning never teleports an actor. See [routing](CONTACTS_AND_ROUTING.md) and [walking regressions](../crates/terra-sandbox/tests/walking_routes.rs).

The final frozen-source replay of the unchanged saved case reduced its planned length from **261.806 m to 75.345 m**, using **2,817 source queries**. The character reached the same goal within normal arrival tolerance in **18.9 physical seconds**. Stepwise checks covered support, contacts, bounded motion, money and goods, with exact save/resume continuation. This is a prepared-source replay, distinct from the native interface observations. The [final replay](verification/v0.13-final/walking-replay.json) reproduced these results with the unchanged generation-251 input and exact prepared-source identity. Its private complete input is excluded from the project; the public geometry regression and replay tool remain included.

A separate integration review found parked vehicles could accept an RTS order and display movement intent while the parking brake held their speed at zero. The successful `MoveUnits` commit now releases that brake only after the entire group, ownership, licenses and range are admitted. Rejected groups change no brakes or orders. Fuel, condition, rental expiry, company assignment, terrain and contacts remain authoritative. [Regression scenarios](../crates/terra-sandbox/tests/rts_vehicle_orders.rs) cover exited, newly rented and company-released vehicles through real bounded steps.

These fixes close reproduced local failures. The existing physical traffic-recovery fixtures are retained and rerun in the release gates; none is replaced with a cleared road or a teleported delivery. Multi-obstacle navigation, dense dynamic networks and reliable unattended operation across arbitrary prepared worlds remain acceptance work.

## Communities, ownership and network authority

Communities now support leaving, removing a member, transferring leadership, declining an invitation and revoking an invitation. Only the current leader manages the roster. A leader must hand over before leaving, the successor must already be a member, and the former leader becomes a Builder. Departing members lose shared access and a home belonging to that faction, including cold/offline references. Personal property, unrelated homes, faction assets and public contributions remain with their existing owners. [Domain lifecycle](../crates/terra-sandbox/src/factions.rs) and [community rules](SETTLEMENTS_AND_ECONOMY.md) describe the contract.

The native Factions panel names the invitation being accepted, pages the roster and reviews the consequences of removal, handover, leaving and permanent plot sharing. Reviews retain the principal, restore revision and faction roster; property sharing also retains ownership identities. Changed context expires the review rather than silently targeting another community or plot. Shared-property creator IDs do not bypass current membership permissions.

The candidate native journey used an isolated two-member fixture. Cancel preserved leader #1. Confirmed handover, save and restore showed leader #2 and the former leader as Builder. This rendered fixture is separate from the actual two-principal TLS integration test. That test exercises invitation, joining, handover, departure, permissions, private state and host save/restart through the transport.

Cross-review found a real replica mismatch: the authority intentionally includes a nearby faction owning visible property, while the client validator formerly accepted only membership or invitation relevance. The validator now accepts the same bounded visible-property context and still rejects unrelated rosters. Private inventories and capabilities remain recipient-specific. [TLS lifecycle](../crates/terra-net/tests/faction_lifecycle_tls.rs) and [replica validation](../crates/terra-net/src/replication.rs) preserve the regression coverage. Inactive-leader succession, dissolution, political systems and territory-reclaim policy remain separate product design work.

## SDK content, operations and delivery

The pass preserves the validated animated wildlife example, character customization, procedural fallback and connected prepared-map bundles. Model-pack validation, independent animation bindings, aggregate scene admission and immutable content identity remain in the release gates. Source-package inspection/export/verification/install preserve coverage and provenance. No new production human, vehicle or audio pack is claimed. Estimated scene budgets do not measure actual GPU memory, and content hashes do not establish publisher trust or redistribution rights. See [asset packs](ASSET_PACKS.md) and [source bundles](SOURCE_BUNDLES.md).

Operational guarantees retain their regional scope: durable storage barriers, bounded interest views, dormant records, verified backups and finite client/recovery workloads. This pass does not replace them with an assertion of WAN capacity. Storage pause tails, sustained load, offsite retention, declared recovery objectives, crash telemetry and public-account policy remain open. The current telemetry export still depends on graceful exit. [Operations](OPERATIONS.md) and the [gap register](REVIEW_GAPS_v0.13.md) keep these boundaries explicit.

The new [compatibility contract](COMPATIBILITY.md) is checked against actual source constants and workspace versions: release **0.13.0**, protocol **10**, ALPN **terra-world/10**, domain save **1**, regional schema **1**. Clients and hosts must upgrade together. Schema continuity preserves compatible saves; it does not authorize changing a world's source or catalog identity.

The desktop packager requires completed gates, unchanged source inputs, the recorded compiler and the exact accepted executable. A curated payload includes original runtime data, licenses, connected player guides and hashed verification evidence; private worlds and identities are excluded. Launcher scripts establish the correct working directory and user-data path. The artifact is an optimized dev-profile review build. The [desktop verification record](desktop-v13-verification.json) identifies the resulting Linux package by hash and records the separate extracted-folder launcher and prepared-world smoke test. Linux/Windows CI configuration is present, but Windows execution, supported hardware, signing, installers and update policy remain unqualified. ## Release judgment and remaining priorities

The intended release closes current documentation drift and establishes narrower, reviewable improvements in a finite career, local navigation and basic community lifecycle. The original report remains part of the project, with every unresolved baseline identifier retained. Next product work should prioritize representative longer player journeys and living costs, broader traffic layouts, physical-device qualification, production content and deployment recovery evidence. Further scope should follow those measured needs; this regional SDK/reference game does not yet establish global multiplayer architecture or a production public service.

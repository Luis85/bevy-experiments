## Boundaries and continuity

An accepted supply promise is not cancelled merely because another system fills demand. Public offer retirement only removes confirmed obsolete demand and only after exact credit to its original issuer. Temporary full receiving capacity retains existing terms; a new offer alone is reduced to what fits. Unknown or missing destination data does not prove deletion. A full issuer retains escrow until it can receive the refund.

Native intent snapshots retain stable operands and desired operation, not continuously changing timers or payroll. They do not reserve a remote transaction. Actual commands still validate authority/reach/capacity; deliberately browsing a new target requires reviewing its action.

Portable file comparison is cooperative protection, not a guarantee against concurrent administrative path replacement or unqualified network filesystems. CI wiring and Linux tests do not establish actual Windows acceptance. Safe ZIP/executable consistency does not establish publisher trust.

Current evidence uses new v0.24 review/preflight/native/acceptance/delivery directories. Earlier evidence remains historical. Failed/interrupted runs never authorize packaging.
